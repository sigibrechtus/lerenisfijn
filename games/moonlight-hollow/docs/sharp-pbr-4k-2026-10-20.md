# 4K Sharp rendering and PBR quality upgrade — 20 October 2026

## Confirmed source-level changes

The 4K Sharp framebuffer budget continues to be 3840 × 2160 maximum (or physical pixel density on mobile). Unlike the previous implementation, desktop Sharp now chooses the high PBR tier and enables a **selective, UV-mapped 4096 × 4096 procedural albedo/normal/ORM texture triplet** for the stone surface family when the browser reports a 4096-capable maximum texture dimension. Other shared material families use 2048 or lower depending on the residency budget. A 4K map is **not** a promise that every object is individually textured at 4K.

Textures are generated from the existing deterministic and tileable surface design system and assigned to Babylon PBRMaterial: albedo=sRGB, tangent-space normal=linear, metallic texture=linear packed AO/Roughness/Metallic (R/G/B). ORM is also explicitly assigned to Babylon's ambientTexture slot to carry the red-channel AO. Anisotropic filtering and mipmaps remain enabled.

The desktop PBR estimated resident limit remains 640 MiB and iOS remains capped to the balanced PBR tier with the existing 128 MiB budget. For the isolated Pumpkin Race/broom scenes, only the **pumpkin** family is eligible for a runtime-generated 4K PBR triplet. On iOS Sharp uses native-density framebuffer output and balanced-quality PBR, not full 4K maps. The material bank preflights resident memory and checks temporary old/new texture overlap during upgrades; estimates exclude driver overhead and other non-texture resources.

Changing graphic quality while the settings dialog closes now changes the PBR tier and 4K eligibility without recreating the scene. The Sharp profile keeps FXAA and glow disabled, avoiding a soft display. The render-resolution cap remains unchanged.

Desktop worlds opened directly in high/Sharp build round props with more sphere segments (36→48 for major silhouettes and 16→24 for smaller ones), and cylinders with 48 rather than 36 tessellation. These mesh subdivisions are authored on scene creation; changing the profile mid-session adjusts texture quality and scenery visibility immediately, while existing mesh topology is unchanged until a world reload.

Sharp now retains full decorative scene meshes and requests the same varied reusable GLB/foliage decoration profile as high (rather than skipping enhancement entirely). Mini-game scenes similarly request high geometry/PBR on capable desktops instead of the previous low profile.

## Performance and QA caveats

- Dynamic 4096-pixel maps can take CPU time and transient RAM to construct. Live timing on desktop WebGL is still required.
- Browser device support is indicated by maxTextureSize but does not guarantee sufficient GPU memory or 4K performance.
- A full asset-by-asset 4K texture library has **not** been generated. Existing Moon Express 4096-pixel map files remain available; locomotive integration and WebGL acceptance are tracked separately.
- Confirm the first-/third-person silhouette quality and exercise readability on actual desktop WebGL and iPhone Safari. Node tests cannot certify visible graphics or measured GPU memory.
- Do not advance the 18-family asset production registry to complete based on these rendering-profile changes alone.
