Stage themes are moon gate, star bridge, whisper forest, pumpkin court, crystal garden and light festival. Stage hops use checkpoint cues and reset held input. Completion requires six successes and is recorded once. These are stage journeys with spatial hops; they are not a single seamless open racing circuit.

Each configured main-world anchor has twelve inhabitants: five cottages, three district buildings, five campaign sites, six stations, six destinations and the village square. Total: 312 creatures at 26 anchors. Each active mini-world has 72 creatures across six areas. Ground walkers use actual terrain or visible floors and avoid gameplay corridors. Distant populations are culled; pause/reduced motion freeze patrols.

Eight original surface families add wood grain, stone joints, roof tiles, brushed metal, pumpkin ribs, cloth weave, earth and plaster relief. Native balanced textures are 1024; HD uses selected 2048 surfaces and Eco 512, subject to hardware limits and bounded mip memory. Normal maps come from height rather than baked lighting. Terrain has 25,921 vertices; important curved silhouettes use 36 rather than 24 sphere segments, cylinders 36 rather than 24 sides, boulders have smoother subdivisions, and cottages have 350 shared-geometry roof shingles.

Campaign feedback uses twelve pooled motes and one fading ring; mini-games retain their bounded choreography pool. Texture banks share maps, preserve colors and replace resources only on tier changes. Native checks verify behavior, geometry, texture pixels and cleanup. Actual device frame rates remain unmeasured.
